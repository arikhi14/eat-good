# Eat Good

Eat Good is a personalized food discovery app designed to help people eat healthier without giving up the foods they already love.

Instead of forcing users into strict meal plans or overwhelming them with nutrition tracking, Eat Good learns about each user's dietary restrictions, health goals, favorite foods, and preferences. It then helps them discover healthier recipes and nearby food options that fit their needs.

## The Idea

Sometimes you don't want to search for "healthy food."

You just want pizza.

Eat Good lets users search:

> **What do you want to eat?**

A user can search for something like pizza, pasta, burgers, chicken wings, tacos, or ice cream.

Eat Good then provides two ways to satisfy that craving:

**Make It** — Find a healthier recipe that respects the user's dietary restrictions and goals.

**Find It** — Discover nearby restaurants that offer compatible versions of that food.

The goal is to make healthier eating feel realistic, personalized, and simple.

## Core Features

### Personalized Onboarding

When users first open Eat Good, they create a personalized food profile.

The onboarding process collects information such as:

- Dietary restrictions
- Health and nutrition goals
- Height
- Weight (optional)
- Activity level
- Foods they are trying to reduce
- Foods they love
- Location
- Relevant health considerations

Dietary preferences may include:

- Gluten-free
- Dairy-free
- Vegetarian
- Vegan
- Nut-free
- Shellfish-free

Goals may include:

- Eat healthier
- Lose weight
- Build muscle
- Improve energy
- Improve mood
- Support brain health

## Personalized Dashboard

Eat Good's home screen is designed to be intentionally simple.

Users will see:

- A search bar asking **"What do you want to eat?"**
- Estimated maintenance calories
- Their current health goal
- Healthier versions of foods they already love
- Personalized food recommendations

Rather than becoming another complicated calorie tracker, Eat Good focuses on helping users decide what to eat.

## Food Search

Users can search for whatever food they are craving.

For example:

```text
Pizza
```

Eat Good will provide both recipe and nearby restaurant results.

### Make It

Users can discover healthier versions of the food they searched for.

For example:

```text
High-Protein Gluten-Free Pizza

520 calories
42g protein

✓ Gluten-Free
✓ High Protein
```

Recommendations are personalized using the user's dietary restrictions, preferences, and goals.

### Find It

Users can also discover nearby restaurants offering compatible versions of the food.

For example:

```text
Pizza Restaurant

0.6 miles away

✓ Gluten-free crust available
✓ Vegetarian options
```

This allows users to decide whether they want to cook something themselves or find it nearby.

## Map

Eat Good includes a map for discovering nearby food options that fit the user's preferences.

Users will be able to explore nearby restaurants and filter locations based on dietary needs such as:

- Gluten-free
- Dairy-free
- Vegan
- Vegetarian
- High-protein options

Restaurant information is intended to help users discover possible options. Eat Good does not guarantee that a restaurant is free from allergens or cross-contamination.

## Profile

Users can update their profile at any time.

Editable information will include:

- Dietary restrictions
- Health goals
- Height
- Weight
- Activity level
- Favorite foods
- Foods they want to reduce
- Location

Updating this information will allow Eat Good to reevaluate and personalize future recommendations.

## Project Goals

Eat Good is being built around a few core principles:

1. **Keep it simple** — Users should be able to understand the app immediately.
2. **Start with what people actually want to eat** — Healthy eating should not mean abandoning favorite foods.
3. **Personalize recommendations** — Dietary restrictions and goals should meaningfully affect results.
4. **Make discovery fast** — Users should be able to go from craving to recipe or restaurant in just a few taps.
5. **Prioritize usability** — The app should feel polished, intuitive, and enjoyable to use.

## Development

Eat Good is currently under active development.

The initial MVP is being developed as a two-week project with the goal of producing a functional and polished iOS application suitable for TestFlight and App Store submission.

## Disclaimer

Eat Good is intended for general wellness and food discovery purposes and is not a substitute for professional medical or nutritional advice.

Users with allergies, celiac disease, or other medical dietary requirements should independently verify ingredients, preparation methods, and potential cross-contamination with restaurants and food providers.
